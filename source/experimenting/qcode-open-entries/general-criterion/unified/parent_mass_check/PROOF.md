# Complete seven-site parent cells from coordinate types

The dimension-three, distance-at-least-five, seven-site additive cell is
regenerated by suffix_mass_check.verify8.type_check: every multiset of seven
coordinate row spaces of dimension at most two in GF(2)^3 is considered. There
are 15 types and 116,280 multisets. The 2,171 distance-five multisets yield
exactly the 32 saved physical graph certificates. Any binary additive code with
three independent generators has such coordinate row spaces; local GL(2,2)
identifies the maps with a fixed row space. Thus no dimension-three code is
missing from this enumeration.

For a binary dimension-k code C, its dimension-(k+1) supercodes correspond
bijectively to the nonzero cosets of C. A supercode has minimum weight at least
five exactly when the coset minimum is at least five. Exhaustively assign all
4^7 physical words to quotient labels and compute these minima. This establishes
each parent's exact number of eligible extensions.

Every dimension-(k+1) binary code has 2^(k+1)-1 hyperplanes. Compute all physical
orbit sizes as 7!6^7 divided by independently encoded site-star automorphism
orders. The full incidence count from the complete dimension-k cell must equal
the orbit mass of all listed dimension-(k+1) children times their hyperplane
count. Listed children are individually checked for dimension, distance and
distinct physical graph certificates.

The verifier also enumerates every hyperplane of every child and matches its
parent graph certificate. The resulting weighted incidence agrees separately
for every parent class, not just in the total. Apply this first to 32 dimension-
three parents and 43 dimension-four children, then to those 43 parents and the
one dimension-five child. These equalities prove both seven-site parent cells
complete without assuming the release16 census is complete.

The exact parent input files are the ones pinned by the existing independently
checked eight-site mass and nine-site lift reports. This certificate supplies
their remaining parent-completeness premise without modifying frozen reports.
Combined with those checks and suffix6_mass_check, it closes the classification
chain used here; it does not exclude [[17,3,6]].
