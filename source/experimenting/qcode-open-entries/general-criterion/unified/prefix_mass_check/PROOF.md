# Independent completeness of local full-kernel candidates

For q=1 a local kernel H has dimension two and contains a distinguished radical
word p of weight eight. Local Pauli letter permutations send p to Z^8. Thus H
is generated by p and some h commuting with p, with both h and h+p of weight
at least six. Enumerate all 4^8 choices of h, identifying h with h+p. This is
an exhaustive enumeration of the distinguished pairs (H,p).

For q=2,3, puncturing a length-eight, dimension-(q+1), minimum-six additive code
preserves its dimension, since a puncture kernel would contain a weight-one
word. The seven-site parent has minimum weight at least five. The independently
complete seven-site dimension-three/four cells are supplied by
parent_mass_check/chain.json. For every parent, test every ordered pair of
binary forms at the new coordinate (64 or 256 pairs). These are all possible
new-coordinate maps, including degenerate ones. Keep minimum-six lifts and
every weight-eight word p in their symplectic radical. Distinguish p in a
site-star physical graph certificate. Compare the complete resulting certificate
sets to the actual planned (H,p) representatives. No tail-form orbit or assumed
list of local weight distributions is needed.

The verifier separately checks every raw capacity profile. In the full
physical eight-site Pauli space it computes every coset minimum modulo H by
exhaustive word assignment. Retain exactly the cosets commuting with p, using
p's coordinates in H^perp to test their syndromes. This is R^perp/H. The
histogram and every capacity threshold must match the saved profile. This
computation does not use the production complement-basis capacity routine.

A PASS certifies the local candidate and capacity inputs, relative to the
already independently checked seven-site parents and physical graph encoding.
It does not close any remaining quantum extension search.
