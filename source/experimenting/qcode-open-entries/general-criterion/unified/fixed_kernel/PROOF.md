# Exact fixed full local kernel

This namespace is independent of the frozen production implementations. It
does not by itself exclude [[17,3,6]]. Its target scope is R=<Z^8>, exact local
centralizer H, and exact suffix stabilizer N. Limits never exclude.

Put p=m-1 and E=Rperp/R, with standard basis Zi, Xi+Xlast (i<p).
Write H/R=K, dim K=q<=3, rank(K)=t in {0,2}, g=q-t. For the target,
dim N=q-1, dim(P/N)=2p-q and its Gram Gamma has radical dimension g.
An exact extension has an injective isometry tau:P/N -> E with image Kperp.
Its adjoint M has kernel exactly K. Every actual target has these properties.

## Necessary Z constraints

The full radical of Gamma maps isomorphically to Krad. All ordered bases of
Krad are enumerated relative to one fixed basis of rad(Gamma): 1,1,6,168
choices for g=0,1,2,3. Every candidate H is checked to contain R, commute
with R, and have H intersect pureZ equal to R. Hence its radical X projection
has rank g; one physical site permutation makes the first g tags independent.
The last tag is the sum of the preceding tags.

For a chosen identification k_j=tau(rho_j), let tag_i[j]=X_i(k_j).
The label xi_i=M(Zi) obeys rho_j dot xi_i=tag_i[j]. Let a_i express tag_i
in the first g tags, and set

    T_i=xi_i + sum_j a_i[j] xi_j,
    zprime_i=Zi + sum_j a_i[j] Zj.

Then T_i is in im(Gamma) and zprime_i commutes with Krad. If t=2, choose
u,v in K with sp(u,v)=1 and project

    e_i=zprime_i + sp(zprime_i,v)u + sp(zprime_i,u)v.

If t=0, put e_i=zprime_i. For any preimage c_i satisfying Gamma c_i=T_i,
tau(c_i) and e_i differ by an element of Krad. Therefore

    c_i dot T_j = sp(e_i,e_j)=F[i,j].

Any right preimage works: their difference is radical, which annihilates
T_j. F has zero anchor rows and rank t. For t=2, failure of rank two would
give a nonzero K element in pureZ, contrary to the explicit intersection
check. Native recursion enforces every tag, every F pair, Z independence,
and every pure-Z distance inequality. The eighth singleton is the dependent
sum of the first seven and is included in those weight inequalities.

## Exact Y completion

For fixed independent Z images, complete their syndrome basis and transform
Gamma by congruence, independently checked against mixed_frames.fixed_z.
The lower right Gram block must vanish. The high coordinates of the Y images
are then fixed; their low coordinates U_i satisfy coupled off-diagonal
equations with free diagonals. For every k=z|(x<<p) in a basis of K, impose

    XOR_{i:x_i=1} U_i = z + XOR_{i:x_i=1} offset_i.

All sector weight inequalities and every subset XOR are tested. At a leaf,
model.criterion and M(H)=0 are asserted. The radical tag matrix has full rank
g, so im(M) together with im(Gamma) spans all 2p-q syndrome dimensions.
Thus rank M=2p-q, its kernel is exactly K, and its suffix kernel is exactly N.
Actual constructions are additionally checked in physical Pauli coordinates.

## Prefix actions and radical-identification orbits

Physical Aut(H,p) preserves the original site weights and the pure-Z
subspace. Its action on all radical identifications can be reduced to
orbits, recording every member. Within an identification, use only the
subgroup fixing each k_j modulo R. Filtering the supplied automorphism
generators and closing those that fix it may yield a proper subgroup of
the full stabilizer; this loses speed but remains sound.

For an independent tuple xi, a nonidentity coefficient mask can never have
the same image as a basis unit. Therefore the lexicographic comparison for
each group action is decided at its first nonidentity row. Keep only that
(row,mask) pair, deduplicate pairs, and test it once its support is assigned.
This gives exactly the same partial rejection as the full action scan.

No suffix first-image orbit reduction is used by the initial pilot: its mask
contains every eligible, tag-correct label. Before introducing a suffix
reduction, require its group to preserve C,N and fix the chosen radical
identification. The certified hull3 runner uses symmetry.suffix_slice under
the following proved combined lex conditions.

## Combined lex conditions

Suppose a prefix subgroup A and a suffix subgroup B each preserve the SAME
fixed-H,N,identification solution slice, and both tests compare integer labels
in the SAME coordinate-wise lexicographic order. Choose a globally lexicographically
least full Z tuple in an orbit of the group generated by A and B. That tuple
is prefix-lex-minimal under A. Its first label must also be least in its B
orbit, since otherwise a B action would lower the full tuple at its first
coordinate. Consequently imposing BOTH prefix lex inequalities and a
minimum first-label orbit representative still retains a tuple from every
joint orbit. This requires neither computing a unique joint canonical form
nor having A fix the first site. It may leave multiple representatives.
The independent final Opus audit accepts this argument and corrects its
earlier blanket warning: opus_workers/fixed_kernel_final_audit/AUDIT.md.

Here slice invariance follows from physical automorphisms, not from sampled
trees. A prefix physical alpha preserves R,H, the metric and symplectic form.
The action actually used is M'(e)=M(alpha e), so its Z columns are exactly
X'_i=XOR_j A_i[j] X_j for A_i=coordinates(alpha Zi). Its adjoint is
tau'=alpha^{-1} tau. Since alpha fixes each identified radical class modulo
R, the identification remains fixed; its inverse also fixes those classes.
The corresponding global code is obtained by a physical alpha^{-1} on the
prefix. Thus the exact kernel, all distance conditions and all Y completions
are transported bijectively.

A suffix physical beta preserves C,N and all site weights. Its induced
syndrome action S is constructed as syndrome(beta representative(s)), exactly
the column orientation used by the code. It acts by M'=S M, corresponding
to the global physical suffix operation beta. Compatibility is preserved
by S Gamma S^T=Gamma. Its adjoint is tau'=tau S^T on quotient coordinates;
the explicit filter S^T rho_j=rho_j fixes each radical identification.
Consequently both subgroups preserve the same complete-extension slice.
Ordering its Z projections suffices even when several Y completions tie.

verify_joint.py independently checks the combined pruning logic on complete
small Z trees using the supplied group actions. Correctness of those actions
rests on physical automorphism verification, the syndrome linearity, distance
and Gram checks, and the physical replay in verify_symmetry.py; the joint
test is not an independent classification of the groups. Proper subgroups
remain sufficient for coverage. The hardcoded distance-five representatives
inside syndrome_orbits are unused; suffix_slice computes its own eligible
set at the actual requested distance.

## Coverage and evidence

verify.py retains the original nondegenerate q2 control. verify_generic.py
compares complete native Z trees with an independent Python oracle and all
physical maps with the frozen full-Y reference, filtering exact full rank.
verify_symmetry.py replays physical identification actions and full S8
canonical/noncanonical controls. verify_large.py constructs true [[17,3,3]]
controls for BOTH q3 types, tests native fixed Z and Y completion, and
enumerates the complete physical centralizer; these are distance THREE
controls, not target witnesses.

Any production result still needs complete, hash-pinned C classification,
all allowed N (or proved physical orbits), every H with distinguished p,
all radical-identification orbits, and unlimited complete Z AND Y searches.
The small-H lists in kernel_capacity/profiles.json inherit the explicitly
recorded critical-prefix parent censuses. The suffix classification has
independent star-graph replay and parent mass verification in
suffix_mass_check/, audited by opus_workers/suffix_mass_audit/AUDIT.md.
